style: make the page one continuous surface

The page flipped between dark and light three times: dark hero, white
body, dark offer panel, white body, dark closing CTA. That reads as a
flicker rather than a design, and it was a fair criticism.

Rule applied: exactly ONE dark region - the hero - then an uninterrupted
light page. The dark block is what stops the scroll and earns the
attention; a second and third one diluted it.

- .offer: dark gradient panel -> light card with a 4px amber top rule,
  so it is distinguished by border and accent rather than by inverting
  the page
- .close: dark panel -> light card, keeping the centred layout
- step counters: solid amber -> soft amber wash with dark amber text
  (matches the new --accent-soft token and keeps contrast)
- added --accent-soft token and a .btn.lg size; the two hero/final CTAs
  use it so the primary action stays the largest thing on the page

Verified after the change: one background:var(--ink) rule remains (the
hero), zero white text nodes left in the body, all div tags balanced
43/43, no encoding damage. Every verified legal claim still present and
the invented deadline and unverified fee figures still absent.
