feat: try-it demo, on-page lead form, founding-client scarcity

Three structural conversion changes, not decoration. The previous pass
added motion to a page whose main leaks were structural.

1. TRY IT (the biggest one)
   Visitors paste their own list and watch it cross-match. Nothing is
   uploaded and nothing leaves the browser - the whole thing runs on the
   page against a small FICTIONAL demo blocklist, and the 3D gate is
   driven with the same numbers via RegistryGate.setRecords(), so the
   animation and the results table can never disagree.
   A sceptic can now see the mechanic in ten seconds instead of taking
   it on faith. The demo blocklist is fictional and labelled as such; no
   real compliance data is implied anywhere.

2. ON-PAGE FORM replaces the calendar bounce
   The primary CTA went to cal.agentcy.co.za, which loses most of the
   people who click it. Replaced with a 7-field form that promises a
   written answer rather than a meeting. lead-form.js validates, then
   POSTs to window.AGENTCY_FORM_ENDPOINT if one is configured, and
   otherwise composes a prefilled mailto: so a lead is never silently
   dropped. It never reports success without a real response, and the
   catch path falls back to mailto rather than losing the enquiry.
   The privacy note is deliberate: this is a compliance service, so
   quietly harvesting details would undercut the whole pitch.

3. FOUNDING-CLIENT SCARCITY
   Starter rate locked for 12 months, audit free either way, rate rises
   once enough clients are onboarded. This is TRUE - there are currently
   zero clients - so the urgency is real rather than manufactured.
