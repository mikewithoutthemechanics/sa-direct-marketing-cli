docs: rebuild one-pager for conversion, keeping the verified legal position

The page was factually correct but did not sell. A compliance page that
leads with regulation asks the reader to do homework before they will
listen. This version leads with the consequence and keeps the citations
in the footer where they belong.

Conversion changes:
- Headline is now the consequence, not the product name:
  "You are still messaging people who asked you to stop." Nobody wakes
  up wanting a cleanse; they do worry about fines.
- Restructured to problem > threat > offer > price > objections > CTA,
  which is the order a cold reader can follow.
- Risk moved above the fold as a dedicated panel instead of a footnote.
- "Free check" is now the primary CTA and is given a three-step "how it
  works", because a free concrete offer converts better than a
  subscription. The subscription is the second ask, after the audit.
- Added an objection-handling section (too small, "we only WhatsApp",
  "isn't this just POPIA", "is this legal advice") using the verified
  facts rather than new claims.
- Three CTAs instead of one, all to cal.agentcy.co.za.
- Visual: dark hero, larger type scale, tagged fact cards, one
  "most popular" tier, mobile breakpoints tightened. Readable on a
  phone, which is how this audience will actually open it.

Honesty retained - this is deliberate and not an oversight:
- No invented deadline. The "phased in, no deadline announced" position
  is kept, and reframed: we will not invent urgency the regulator has
  not created. It now argues for acting early rather than against.
- Unverified NCC fee figures still not quoted; passed through at cost
  and confirmed at onboarding.
- "Not legal advice" now appears in the objection section, where a
  sceptical prospect will actually look for it.
- No social proof or client counts, because there are none yet. Nothing
  on this page is fabricated.

Verified after rewrite: all false claims still absent, all verified
claims still present (B2B scope, opt-in override, industry blocks, the
R1m/10% penalty, the 15 Apr 2026 effective date, phased-in timing, fees
at cost, not-legal-advice). Tags balanced 43/43, no encoding damage.
