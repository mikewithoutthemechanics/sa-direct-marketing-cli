feat: GSAP motion layer, vendored and safely degradable

Adds animation, with restraint: this page asks a sceptical reader to
trust Agentcy with a legal exposure, so motion is used to guide the eye
down the argument rather than to entertain.

Animations:
- hero staggers in on load (5 elements, 0.11s apart)
- an amber rule draws itself under the emphasised word in the H1
- 22 elements reveal on scroll via ScrollTrigger, once each
- the three "cost you" facts animate as a group with a stagger
- the R1,000,000 figure counts up when it scrolls into view, then
  settles on the exact number
- the highlighted pricing tier pops with a slight overshoot
- the offer CTA pulses once - deliberately not a loop

Correctness and resilience:
- GSAP 3.12.5 is VENDORED to docs/vendor/, not loaded from a CDN, so the
  page has no third-party runtime dependency and works offline
- the script opens with `if (!window.gsap) return;` and only then adds
  the `gsap` class to <html>; every hidden state is scoped to `html.gsap`
  (verified: zero unscoped .reveal{opacity:0} rules). If the library
  fails to load, JS is disabled, or the file is opened from somewhere
  the vendor path breaks, the page renders complete and static
- prefers-reduced-motion is respected and short-circuits the whole
  machinery, including clearing the hidden states
- ScrollTrigger.refresh() on window load so late font/asset settling
  does not leave triggers misaligned
