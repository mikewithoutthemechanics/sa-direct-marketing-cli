fix: the gate caption was covering the diagram it describes

The caption was an absolutely positioned overlay pinned to the bottom of
the canvas, with a white gradient over the last few pixels. It was
sitting directly on top of two things the visitor needs to see:

- the CHECKED / SUPPRESSED counters, which the engine draws at the
  bottom of the canvas
- the last few rows of the DNC register

So the caption was obscuring exactly the evidence it was explaining.

Changes:
- .gate-cap is no longer an overlay. It sits in normal flow beneath the
  canvas with a top border and a surface tint, so it reads as a caption
  to the diagram rather than something floating over it.
- the copy was also stale: it still described the deleted 3D gate
  ("blocked records are caught and suppressed, the rest pass through").
  Rewritten to describe what the sweep actually shows - contacts
  crossing the amber line, blocked ones moving into the register.
  This had survived a previous edit because the replacement string
  spanned a line break and silently failed to match.
- the HUD is inset 12px from the bottom edge instead of 6px, so
  descenders are not clipped on a low-DPI screen
- the register now reserves a 34px footer band so its rows always stop
  above the counters rather than running into them

Verified: caption carries no position:absolute; all tags balanced (body
details 2/2, the apparent third is the literal word in a CSS comment);
no encoding damage; engine invariants still hold over 900 steps and in
demo mode (6 checked, 3 suppressed, 3 cleared; register holds every
blocked number and no cleared ones). Legal claims unchanged.
