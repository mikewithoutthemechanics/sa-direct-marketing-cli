fix: four defects found in review, one of them serious

1. CRITICAL - the try-it demo was not labelled as simulated
   A visitor could paste a real list, see contacts marked BLOCKED in
   red, and reasonably believe those people had actually blocked them.
   Nothing anywhere on the page said the blocklist was fictional.
   This was self-inflicted: an earlier pass trimmed the try-it lede
   from 34 words to 10 and discarded the second sentence, which was
   the one disclosing the fictional sample list. A compliance service
   displaying a fabricated result unlabelled is the worst failure mode
   this page could have.
   Fixed with a prominent, unmissable notice immediately above the
   input - not fine print - stating that the results are a
   demonstration against a short fictional sample list, NOT the live
   NCC registry, and that the real answer comes in writing.

2. Both CTAs still bounced to cal.agentcy.co.za
   The hero "Check my list - free" and the offer "Book the 10-minute
   check" were the two highest-intent clicks on the page and both left
   for a calendar scheduler. A previous commit message claimed the
   calendar bounce had been replaced; that overstated what had been
   done - the form existed at the bottom, but the two CTAs above it
   still leaked out. Both now anchor in-page: the hero CTA to the
   try-it demo, the offer CTA to the form.

3. Orphaned duplicate text after the FAQ
   Two answer paragraphs were left visible outside the <details>, after
   the regex that converted the FAQ had already moved copies of them
   inside. Removed.

4. Stale aria-label and a fabricated badge
   The canvas aria-label still described the deleted 3D gate
   ("falling through a rotating registry gate"). Rewritten to describe
   the sweep. The Growth tier's "Most popular" badge was fabricated -
   there are no clients. Changed to "Best value", which is supportable
   from the published tier rates.

Also fixed during this pass: my CSS insertion consumed the closing
</style> tag, which would have left the entire page unstyled. Caught by
a tag-balance check rather than by eye, and restored.
