refactor: cut the page roughly in half, and polish the sweep motion

The page had grown to 1037 words through successive passes. It had
become a document instead of a page, and the reading burden was the
biggest conversion leak on it.

Reading load
- 1037 -> 730 words as a reader actually encounters them (984 if
  everything is expanded), measured by stripping script/style and
  excluding collapsed details.
- The three "cost you" cards became three numbered rows: same three
  facts, ~55 words fewer, and a list scans where cards do not.
- The legal position and the FAQ moved into native details. No JS, no
  dependency, keyboard accessible, collapsed by default - so the
  careful "phased in, no deadline announced" positioning is still
  present and still honest, just not read by people who do not need it.
- Try-it lede cut from 34 words to 10; it was repeating the caption.
- Offer blurb, founding block and footer trimmed.
- Removed the CSS for .problem, .qa and .note.warn, which no longer have
  any markup behind them.

Motion polish
- records now ease into motion with a smoothstep ramp instead of
  snapping to speed, which is what made the first version mechanical
- the catch uses easeOutCubic, so a record leaves briskly and settles
  into its register slot rather than sliding linearly
- a brief amber glow at the moment of capture draws the eye to the
  catch instead of making the reader hunt for it
- register entries arrive with a wipe that decays, so the right-hand
  column visibly RECEIVES the record, which is the entire point of it

Bugs fixed while editing
- my own regex edits left two orphaned closing divs after the FAQ was
  converted, and the first details insertion left the block unbalanced.
  Found with a div-stack scan rather than a count, since a count alone
  does not tell you where the damage is. Now 48/48 with zero strays.

Verified: checked === suppressed + cleared still holds over 900 steps;
demo mode still settles on exactly the pasted list (6 checked, 3
suppressed, 3 cleared); register contains every blocked number and no
cleared ones; all three page scripts pass node --check; all assets
resolve; every tag balanced; no encoding damage. Legal claims unchanged
and still verified present, with the invented deadline and unverified
fee figures still absent.
