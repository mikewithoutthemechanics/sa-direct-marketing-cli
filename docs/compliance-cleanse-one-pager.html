Compliance Cleanse

South Africa now has a national opt-out registry. If you email, SMS or WhatsApp a consumer, or a business, you are in scope — and the registry lets them block you by industry, permanently, without ever telling you. We handle the monthly cleanse and keep the dated evidence, so you can show you did it.

If you think you're exempt

  • B2B outreach is covered. The 2026 Amendment Regulations apply to promotional activity aimed at companies, not just consumers. POPIA s69 applies regardless of CPA classification. This is the most common misunderstanding we meet.
  • An opt-out overrides consent. Someone who registers a pre-emptive block must not be marketed to again — even if they opted in with you before.
  • Monthly cleansing is mandatory before you launch campaigns.
  • Registration is required. A direct marketer that has not registered with the NCC is barred from contacting consumers for direct marketing.
  • There is no API. The registry is a web portal, so the export and cross-match is manual — and that is exactly where businesses fall over.
The NCC has confirmed implementation is phased in with no compliance deadline announced yet, and further guidance is expected. We are not quoting you a deadline because the regulator has not set one. Penalties under the CPA can reach a fine of up to R1 million or 10% of turnover.
