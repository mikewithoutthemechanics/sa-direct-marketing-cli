Compliance Cleanse

The CPA 2026 opt-out registry is live. From September 2026 every business that does direct marketing in South Africa must register with the NCC, cleanse its lists monthly, and prove it. We run the whole obligation for you — so a fine of up to R1 million or 10% of turnover never lands.

The obligation (and the risk)

  • NCC registration is mandatory for all direct marketers — initial R2,574, then R1,930.50/yr.
  • Monthly cleanse of your database against the opt-out registry — 12c per record per check.
  • No API exists. The registry is a web portal; the manual export + cross-match is where businesses fall over.
  • Penalty: up to R1M or 10% of annual turnover for marketing to a blocked consumer.
