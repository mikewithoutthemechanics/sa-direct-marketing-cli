Fair questions

“We are small — would anyone come after us?”
The penalty is a flat fine of up to R1m or 10% of turnover, not a share of your spend. One campaign to a blocked consumer is enough. Size is not a defence.

“We do WhatsApp, not marketing.”
Promotional messages to a list are direct marketing, including follow-ups and quote requests to people who did not ask. Most businesses pass this test.

“Isn’t this just POPIA again?”
Different problem, same shape. The registry is new, it is a government portal, and it has no API — so the export and cross-match is manual. That step is where businesses fall over.
